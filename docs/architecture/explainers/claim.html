The life of a claim

What happens between circling a word and seeing it charted: the game’s outbox, the sector’s checks, the race to the insert, the answers, and the copy to the survey’s log. The word, the map and every limit are the real ones.

Why one thread decides
Every claim on this patch of the map goes through one Durable Object, which runs one message at a time. A race is settled by a plain INSERT … ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING, with no locks and nothing to retry.

    
Why batches
Sending each claim to every socket costs players × claims sends, which grows with the square of the crowd. Batching for 200 ms keeps it to about five frames a second for each player, however busy the sector.

    
Why an alarm
A Durable Object holding a timer can’t hibernate, and is billed while it waits. An alarm lets it sleep and wakes it if it was evicted, so the log can trail by up to 30 s at no cost.
