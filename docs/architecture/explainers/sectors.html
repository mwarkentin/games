Letterfield · the shared survey

Sectors and sockets

The survey's map is cut into sectors of 4 × 4 territories, and every sector is its own Durable Object on Cloudflare. Your game holds a WebSocket to at most four of them: the four nearest the middle of your screen. Drag the map and watch it join and leave them.

A sector

192 × 192 cells: 16 territories. One thread with its own SQLite storage holds their claims and the sockets of everyone looking at them, up to 750. Only one message runs at a time, so the first to claim a word wins with a plain INSERT … ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING.

Joining

The game joins the sectors in view nearest the middle of the screen, at most four (a sector with a word of yours still to claim comes next). One that drops out of view stays joined for four seconds, so a little panning doesn't reconnect.

Zoomed out

At 2 pixels a cell a wide screen shows dozens of sectors but joins four, so far-off claims don't arrive live. You can only circle words from 9 pixels a cell up, where a screen covers a sector or two.

The universe

Sectors −12 to 12 each way, 625 in all, hide 1,119,752 words. They report to one Atlas for the world's totals. Out past the edge the map goes on, but those sectors don't report.
