    Doodle Chomp

    Steer Nib around the worksheet and chomp every number that fits the rule. A wrong chomp costs a life, and so does bumping into a doodle.

    Pick a game

    Sums start with numbers up to 5 and grow to 10. Count the dots under each one: blue dots get added, and crossed-out dots get taken away. Nib has 5 lives, and the rule is read out loud.

      How to play
      • Move with the arrow keys or W A S D, swipe the board, tap a square, or use the pad.
      • Chomp with Space or Enter, by tapping Nib, or with the Chomp button.
      • Watch the edges. A red ! shows where a doodle is about to walk in.
      • Listen for the doodles. Their footsteps get louder as they get closer to Nib.
      • Clear the level by chomping every number that fits. You get an extra life every 3 levels.
      • Make the top 10 for your game and difficulty to put your initials on the high-score board, or start a group for family or friends.
