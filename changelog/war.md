## 2026-11-05

- Music: five public-domain pieces played on the chip voices of an old game
  console. The Entertainer plays in the menu, the Thunder and Lightning polka
  at the table, and In the Hall of the Mountain King when a war breaks out,
  getting faster with a double or triple war. Winning gets the end of The
  Stars and Stripes Forever, and losing gets a funeral drum. Turn it off with
  the note beside the sound button.
- Seven deck themes: Classic, Neon, 8-bit, Royal, Space, Spooky and Candy,
  each with its own card backs, faces and face-card pictures. Pick one from
  the menu, or from the pause menu mid-game. Everyone sees their own deck,
  even in a room.
- Play with friends: start a room from the menu and share its four-character
  code or invite link. The host can fill empty seats with bots, and anyone
  who arrives late watches until the next game.

## 2026-11-03

- Wars are bigger: you slam every card you put down in a war, up to three
  face down and then the decider, and each one hits harder than the last.
- Three ways to slam, picked in the menu or at the table: Hold as before,
  Mash (tap as fast as you can) or Fling (throw a card off your deck).
- Add War to your Home Screen and it opens full screen like an app, and
  solo games work offline.

## 2026-11-02

- War: Slam Edition is here. It's the card game War, with slams that go from
  "boop" to "COSMIC SLAM" and eleven power-ups to unlock, none of which change
  the outcome. Play against up to 49 bots, in a game of 20, 60 or 150 battles
  or in total war to the last card.
- A live win probability under the battle counter, which slams never move,
  and a chart of your chances at the end of the game.
- History, on the menu, lists your last 30 games on this device.
