## 2026-11-05

- A global leaderboard. Shifts are ranked by aircraft handled, then by time
  to spare on a clean shift, with boards for each shift and radar sweep.
- Views for Today, This week, This month and All time, with This week to
  start.
- Private groups for family and friends: start one, or join with a
  four-character code or an invite link. Groups and initials are shared with
  the site's other leaderboard games.

## 2026-11-03

- Add Air Traffic Controller to your Home Screen and it opens full screen
  like an app, and works offline.

## 2026-10-29

- A shift in progress is saved as you play. Reload, or come back after your
  phone closes the tab, and it picks up where you left off, paused.
- After a clean shift, the report offers the next shift up, at the same
  radar sweep.

## 2026-10-28

- Air Traffic Controller is here: keep aircraft separated while you land
  arrivals and hand off departures, a remake of the 1985 DOS game.
