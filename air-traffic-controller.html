Air Traffic Controller

Two airports, two navaids and ten fixes. Every aircraft that checks in is yours until it lands or leaves the sector.

Shift
Radar sweep
How to play
  • Select an aircraft by tapping its target or its flight strip, then give an instruction from the deck. Pilots act on the next radar sweep (15 s of game time).
  • Arrivals (strips bound for α or β) land by crossing their airport at altitude 0 on the runway heading: α southbound, β northbound. Approach turns a plane onto final at the navaid lined up with that runway. Land descends it to 0 and hands it to the tower for good.
  • Departures and overflights (bound for fix 0–9) must leave the sector over that fix at 5,000 ft. Departures wait at their airport until you give them a climb altitude.
  • Separation: aircraft at the same altitude stay 3 miles apart, and no aircraft climbs or descends through another within 3 miles. Red marks warn of a loss within 45 s; one loss ends the shift.
  • Moves: jets fly 1 mile per sweep, props 1 mile every second sweep. Altitude changes 1,000 ft per mile. Turns go 45° per mile, starting one mile after the instruction. Arrivals hold at the first navaid they reach unless told otherwise.
  • Fuel: jets carry 15 minutes, props 21, counted from when the strip appears.
Keyboard: type a callsign, then A0–A5, L1–L4, R1–R4, P, H, M, CA or CB. Space advances one sweep, Esc pauses. Rules follow the 1985 DOS game; the sector map is redrawn for a portrait screen.
